## Acquisition proposal

Public Telegram feeds: extract dated event posts and their direct permalink, then validate organizer ticket/venue page for year, time, price, address, cancellation and availability. Maintain both discovery permalink and primary URL. De-duplicate by organizer + venue + normalized title + occurrence date/time; aliases such as Afisha London website and Telegram count as one provider.

Instagram/Threads: first verify official account ownership; use accessible dated posts as discovery, then verify primary program. Public profile alone cannot create an event. Stories, private accounts, closed chats and failed previews do not establish coverage.

Places stay separate from dated events. Beer prices need menu/product size/currency/source date; a £2.50 one-third-pint cannot be presented as a £2.50 pint. Public newspaper announcement of a future bar opening does not establish that it is open today.
