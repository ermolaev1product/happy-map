What the statuses mean
  • Selected listings imported: specific dated events added, not the entire program.
  • Venue and menu checked: a permanent place verified, not an invented daily event.
  • Website or posts read: source researched; upcoming dates may still be unconfirmed.
  • Profile checked or official link: identity or organizational link confirmed, not a current schedule.
  • Search result or directory candidate: needs direct reading and primary-source verification.
  • Old posts, temporary closure or rejected source: not evidence for the current week.
Freshness and coverage
  • Listings preserve the year, Europe/Paris local time, source and verification date. Past events are not moved into a new week. Events that have ended today are hidden when an end time is known.
  • Cancellations are excluded. Full or closed registration is shown in event cards. Recheck with the organizer before going.
  • Duplicates are combined by date, time and venue. Distinct sessions remain separate when dates or times differ.
  • Coordinates come from official maps or verified addresses. Unknown coordinates stay empty; no invented pin is shown.
  • Next: repeatable city and venue imports with cancellation and registration checks, followed by verified dated social posts. Access restrictions are respected.
  • No verified local Threads sources yet. Private chats and posts have not been read.
