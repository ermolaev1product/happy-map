How to install and update
  1. Download the APK and open the file on Android.
  2. If prompted, allow this browser to install the app. You can turn that permission off after installation.
  3. Open Happy Map and choose a city from the menu. Install future APK updates over the existing app to keep your plans.

How to add to your Home Screen
  1. Open this map in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The Happy Map icon will appear on your Home Screen.

Good to know

Maps and fresh listings need an internet connection. Plans are saved on the device where you add them.
