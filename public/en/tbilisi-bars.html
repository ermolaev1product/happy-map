How friends will meet here

Proposed flow. This demonstration does not enable registration or shared meetups on the published website.

1. Start with the mapBrowse the city and choose a place without an account. Sign-in is available in the header.
2. «I will be here»Choose a day, time and visibility. If you are not signed in, use Google or an email link. After signing in, return to your selected place.
3. Friends join youMutual friends can see the plan and join. This arranges a meetup; it does not book a table or buy a ticket.

Visible to friends only by default. “I am here” check-ins expire after two hours. No background tracking. You can remove a check-in or cancel a future plan.
