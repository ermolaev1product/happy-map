What the original version 2.2.0 audit covered
  • Primary and additional links were checked across all events and places in version 2.2.0. The primary source is counted once per listing. Additional links may overlap.
  • Tbilisi: 1,010 listings for 8–14 October, including 855 cinema screenings and 155 other events; 18 places. The screenings are scheduled showtimes, not 855 different films.
  • Belgrade: 180 events and 9 places; Moscow: 150 events and 7 places. These snapshots cover 7–13 October.
  • 81 Moscow KudaGo records are outdated: last successfully checked on 4 October. A refresh attempt on 7 October returned HTTP 403.
  • Yandex Maps currently verifies only MESTO’s address in Tbilisi: 13 events and 1 place. There is no bulk import of bars from Yandex. The map uses OpenStreetMap.
  • Finding a link, chat profile or archived post does not mean current listings have been reviewed. Each source shows its verification status.
  • This is an expanded working directory, not a claim of complete coverage across all three cities. Source review dates may differ from individual event verification dates.
