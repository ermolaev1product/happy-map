What the statuses mean
  • Selected announcements imported: specific dated events have been added, not the entire programme.
  • Venue and menu verified: a permanent place was checked; no fictional daily event was created.
  • Website or posts read: the source has been reviewed; future dates may still be unconfirmed.
  • Profile checked or official link found: affiliation has been confirmed, not the current programme.
  • Search result or directory candidate: direct reading and verification of the primary source are required.
  • Old posts, temporary closures or rejected sources do not confirm the current week’s programme.
Freshness and coverage
  • Announcements retain the year, local Europe/Berlin time, source and verification date. Past events are not carried over to a new week. Events that have ended today are hidden when their end time is known.
  • Cancelled events are excluded. Full or closed registration is marked in the listings. Check the information with the organizer again before attending.
  • Duplicates are merged by date, time and venue. Separate sessions are retained when dates or times differ.
  • Some coordinates indicate a verified postcode area rather than an exact entrance; approximate locations are labelled in the listing. Unknown coordinates are left blank, with no map marker.
  • Next: repeatable imports of city calendars and venue programmes, checking cancellations and registration, followed by verified, dated social media posts. Access restrictions are respected.
  • No local Threads sources have been verified yet. Private chats and posts were not read.
